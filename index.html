Questions, answered

Is CVCraft really free?

Yes. It's free for now: no card, no trial period and no watermark on what you download.

Do you keep my details?

The builder runs in your browser and doesn't upload what you type. It also doesn't save between visits, so download your file before you close the tab.

Will applicant tracking systems read my resume?

For a text-based file that screening software can read, use the Print button and choose Save as PDF. The Download PDF button produces a file that looks identical but is image-based, so its text can't be selected or searched.

What if my CV is too long for one page?

It scales down to fit a single A4 sheet automatically. A very long CV will shrink noticeably, so trim a little if the text starts to look small.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. On small screens the builder splits into Edit and Preview tabs, and the page scales to fit your screen.
